# ALIMS — Investor One-Pager & Deck Outline

## One-pager
**Company:** ALIMS — the Living Atlas of Knowledge. Verified academic knowledge infrastructure for the Global South, starting with Nigeria.
**Problem:** Nigeria graduates millions of research outputs into cupboards and lost laptops; employers cannot verify graduate claims; institutions now carry a *mandatory* national deposit obligation (NERD, NYSC-linked since Oct 2025; 400k+ deposits in year one) with spreadsheets.
**Solution:** Institution-led SaaS: governed deposit → supervision workflow → institutional verification → QR/W3C-verifiable records (NXR-ID) → contribution ledger + academic lineage graph → consented industry activation. Deposit once; NERD-compliant automatically.
**Why now:** policy mandate + fake-degree crisis + employers hiring across borders + cheap AI enrichment rails (ORCID/Crossref/OpenAlex).
**Market:** Nigeria ~300 universities + ~120 polytechnics/colleges, ~2.5M tertiary students; every finalist deposits yearly. SAM (NG tertiary, 5-yr): ₦9–14B/yr SaaS + verification/API. Expansion: Ghana, Kenya, South Africa (product already models them).
**Business model:** Institution SaaS ₦1.8M pilot → ₦3.6M/yr Growth (students free) · employer verify API packs · company challenge/intro credits · national/consortium licences.
**Moat:** consented, verified *lineage dataset* (who supervised, contributed, continued, built-on — with evidence levels), institution workflow lock-in at the exact moment of national compliance, and trust positioning (no integrity scores, no automated accusations) that lecturers defend publicly.
**Traction (prototype):** full PRD loop live; 5-role demo; Tier S/A/B features implemented behind provider adapters (NERD/Turnitin/Paystack/Termii/ORCID); waitlist capturing pilot interest.
**Ask:** Pre-seed ₦150–250M (≈$100–170k) — production foundation (Postgres, files, auth, integrations), 3–5 paid pilots, team (2 eng, 1 partnerships, support). Milestones: 3 paying institutions, 25k verified records, first employer API revenue in 9 months.
**Team:** [Founders — fill: product/engineering + university partnerships]. Advisors to recruit: a serving university librarian, a registrar, an NUC-facing policy voice.
