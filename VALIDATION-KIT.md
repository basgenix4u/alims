# ALIMS Validation Sprint Kit (2 weeks, before production spend)

## Goal & pass bar
Validate that (a) students will deposit without being forced twice, (b) supervisors will review faster than email/WhatsApp threads, (c) a registry will trust the verification, (d) an employer can verify unaided. **Pass:** ≥70% task success unassisted, supervisor turnaround intent ≥4/5, at least 2 institutions sign pilot-interest letters.

## Recruiting (use the landing waitlist — now live at the CTA)
5 students/finalists · 3 supervisors · 2 registry/library staff · 2 employers/HR · 1 company innovation lead. Incentive: airtime + "Founding Researcher" passport badge at launch.

## Interview guides (30 min, record with consent)
**Students:** Walk me through where your project lives today. What happens to it after graduation? Show me the last time you needed to prove you did this work. What would make you *not* upload it? (Listen for: privacy, fear of theft, effort.)
**Supervisors:** Show me how you track who is at which chapter. Where do revisions get lost? What would make you distrust a similarity report? What evidence do you need at external examination?
**Registry/Library:** Walk me through certificate correction/revocation today. What does NUC/NERD ask that you cannot produce quickly? What must never become public?
**Employers:** Show me how you'd verify this certificate today. What would make you stop trusting it? Would you pay per verification or per hire?

## Usability tasks (on the live demo, think-aloud)
1. (Student) Create a record and submit it for review. 2. (Public) Verify `NXR-2025-01041` and explain what was/wasn't proven. 3. (Supervisor) Return a record for revision with a reason. 4. (Admin) Verify a record and find its certificate status. 5. (Company) Find cassava research and request an introduction without seeing any private contact.
Score each: success / assisted / failed + time + verbatim quote.

## Metrics sheet (fill per participant)
Task success %, time-on-task, SUS (10-question) target ≥75, "would use weekly" 1–5, top friction quote, feature request (verbatim). Roll up in one page: keep / fix-before-pilot / kill.

## Landing validation (already instrumented)
Pilot waitlist (role-segmented) at the CTA · demo-role click-through (5 accounts) · verify-widget usage. Targets: ≥100 waitlist in 30 days, ≥25% demo starts, ≥10 employer verify-widget uses from shared links.

## Decision gate (end of week 2)
Proceed to production foundation if pass bar met; otherwise fix the top 3 frictions and re-test with 5 fresh participants. Kill criteria: supervisors refuse the queue even with WhatsApp nudges, or institutions insist NERD alone suffices *and* will not pilot free.
