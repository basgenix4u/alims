# ALIMS Pilot Sales Kit
### Walk into the VC/Registrar's office with everything in this file.

## 1. The 30-second pitch
"NERD made thesis deposit mandatory for every graduate — and your institution now carries that compliance burden with spreadsheets and departmental drives. ALIMS is the campus operating system for that mandate: students deposit once, supervisors review in one queue, your registry verifies and issues QR-verifiable certificates, and your NERD sync, supervision evidence and accreditation reports generate themselves. Pilot one faculty in 30 days; if your finalists aren't depositing through ALIMS, you pay nothing."

## 2. Seven-minute demo script (use the live demo accounts)
1. **Student (amara@demo.alims)** — open the Cassava AI record: version history, embargo, contribution ledger. *"Nothing verified yet is ever labelled verified."*
2. **Public verify (landing page)** — enter `NXR-2025-01041`, then the superseded UNN certificate. *"Any employer, anywhere, in two seconds — and see what we deliberately don't show: grades, IDs, embargoed files."*
3. **Supervisor (supervisor@demo.alims)** — Review Queue, return a record with a reason. *"Similarity is a private signal; a human always decides. No lecturer is ever auto-accused."*
4. **Admin (admin@demo.alims)** — complete verification; certificate issues live; open **NERD Compliance**: readiness bars per institution. *"This screen is your NUC/NERD audit, always current."*
5. **Lineage** — Yoruba MT (2023) → Cassava AI (2025) → AgriServe challenge. *"This is the asset your alumni office and TETFUND reports wish they had."*
6. **Insights** — supervision load + research-gap heatmap. *"Earned-allowance evidence and accreditation data, without a single spreadsheet."*
Close: "One faculty, one semester, your branding, your policies. Shall we scope the pilot?"

## 3. One-page proposal (leave-behind)
- **Problem:** mandatory national deposit + lost/fragmented student research + unverifiable graduate claims.
- **Solution:** ALIMS institution workspace — workflow, verification, certificates, NERD sync, lineage, activation.
- **Pilot scope:** 1 faculty, up to 500 finalists, supervisor + registry training (2 sessions), historic digitisation starter batch (200 records), success review at day 30/60/90.
- **Success metrics (agreed upfront):** ≥80% of pilot finalists deposited; median submission→verification ≤21 days; ≥95% metadata completeness; supervisor satisfaction ≥4/5.
- **Price:** Pilot ₦1.8M (credited against Year-1 subscription). Growth ₦3.6M/yr (2,000 seats). Students/researchers always free.
- **Trust terms:** institution owns its data; export anytime (open formats + API); NDPR-aligned DPA; embargoes and IP boundaries enforced in-product.

## 4. Objection handling
- *"We already have DSpace/a portal."* → "Keep it as the archive if you wish — ALIMS is the workflow and evidence layer in front of it, and syncs. Your students and supervisors live in ALIMS; the library keeps its repository."
- *"NERD will do this for free."* → "NERD is the national registry — it receives deposits. It doesn't run your supervision queue, revisions, contribution disputes, certificates or employer introductions. We make you compliant *and* excellent; then we sync."
- *"Lecturers won't adopt another system."* → "They answer WhatsApp. Review nudges, escalations and one-tap decisions are designed around that. The pilot measures supervisor turnaround weekly — you'll see adoption in data, not promises."
- *"What about plagiarism liability?"* → "Similarity is a private review signal; final decisions are human, reasoned and audited. ALIMS never publishes an integrity score or an accusation — that's in the product, not just the contract."
- *"Data security?"* → "Role-based access enforced server-side, audit history, embargo controls, export-anytime. NDPR DPA signed before any real student data moves."

## 5. Pilot MOU (template — have counsel review)
**Parties:** [Institution] and ALIMS Ltd. **Term:** 90 days from kickoff. **Scope:** one faculty, ≤500 student seats, supervisor/registry/librarian roles, NERD-format sync in simulation until national credentials are issued. **Fees:** ₦1,800,000 pilot fee, credited in full against the first annual subscription if the institution proceeds within 30 days of pilot review. **Data:** Institution retains all rights in its records and data; ALIMS processes solely on documented instructions; export in CSV/JSON + PDF certificates on request and at termination; deletion certificate within 30 days of termination. **Confidentiality & IP:** discovery on ALIMS grants no rights in any record; contact only via permissioned introductions. **Success review:** metrics in §3 measured jointly at days 30/60/90; either party may exit at day 90 with data export. **Liability:** ALIMS evidences workflow/verification facts; academic judgments remain the Institution's. Signatures: VC/Registrar · ALIMS Director · Date.

## 6. 30-day rollout plan
Week 1: branding, departments/programmes, roles, policies (embargo, workflow). Week 2: supervisor + registry training; migrate current finalist list. Week 3: deposits open; daily support desk; NERD-readiness baseline. Week 4: first verifications + certificates; day-30 review against metrics.
